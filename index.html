My Process

How I turn data
into decisions.

A repeatable 5-step workflow from raw data to stakeholder-ready insight — refined through 10+ projects & live internship work.

01
Understand

Clarify the business question, define KPIs, and align with stakeholder goals before touching a single dataset.

02
Extract & Clean

Pull from SQL / APIs / CSVs. Profile, clean & reshape with Python or Power Query for reliable analysis.

03
Explore (EDA)

Run statistical EDA — distributions, correlations, outliers — to find the patterns that actually matter.

04
Model & Visualize

Build star-schema models, DAX measures, interactive dashboards — or ML models when prediction adds value.

05
Storytell

Present insights with narrative, visuals & recommendations that drive action from leadership & ops teams.
